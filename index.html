WHAT WE OFFER

At Zuris beauty barber and spa, we believe beauty is more than appearance — it’s confidence, relaxation, and self‑care. Our salon, spa, and barber shop were created to give every client a space where style meets serenity. From precision haircuts and rejuvenating spa treatments to expert grooming and skincare, our team blends creativity with care to bring out your best look. We pride ourselves on using top‑quality products, modern techniques, and personalized attention for every guest. Step into our world of comfort and elegance — where every visit leaves you refreshed, confident, and ready to shine.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What services do you offer? >

We offer sallon&barber services and spa treatments tailored to relaxation, styling, and personal care.

How do I book an appointment? >

You can book an appointment online through our website or call us directly. We also accept walk-ins subject to availability.

Do you offer gift cards? >

Yes, gift cards are available and make the perfect gift for anyone who loves beauty and relaxation.

What payment methods do you accept (cash, card, mobile money)? >

We accept cash, major cards, and mobile money (M‑Pesa). This makes it easy and convenient for all clients.

How can I reschedule or cancel my appointment? >

Simply call us or use our online booking system at least 24 hours before your appointment, and we’ll be happy to reschedule or cancel for you.
